Equipment Parameters

Monthly volume is sessions per day x working days per week x 52 weeks / 12 months. Every figure here is yours: nothing is filled in for you.

2

The Running Costs Nobody Budgets

A machine that is cheap to buy is often not cheap to own. Account for electricity, scheduled replacement parts, and wear components alongside session consumables.

Wear parts (drive cams, springs, gaskets) require periodic replacement. The calculator divides this cost across the interval months to establish an ongoing monthly reserve, ensuring your payback calculation reflects true maintenance overhead.
3

Time Saved as Money

Count time savings only if you value your time with an explicit hourly rate. A blank rate means no monetary value is counted.

Minutes saved per procedure from faster equipment. To keep financial estimates rigorous, time savings are only converted to cash if you specify an hourly rate below. Leaving the rate blank reports hours saved without inflating cash payback.
